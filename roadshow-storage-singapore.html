How It Works

Your Roadshow, Stop After Stop

You send the venue list once. We run the cycle below for every stop until your programme ends.

Step 1

Collect & Store

We take in your booth, LED units and props, document every part and keep them in our 600-pallet warehouse.

Step 2

Deliver to Venue

Before each stop we pick your assets and deliver to the venue's loading bay within its access window.

Step 3

Set Up & Run

Our crew assembles your booth to the same spec as last time, so it looks identical at every venue.

Step 4

Tear Down & Return

After the stop we dismantle, check for damage, and bring everything back — or straight on to the next venue.

Steps 2–4 repeat for every venue on your list.

Roadshow Logistics in Singapore — Why One Partner Matters

A roadshow that moves through eight malls in three months is not eight separate events. It's one programme with the same booth, the same brand standards and the same budget. When each stop is handled by a different vendor — or the booth sits in someone's office storeroom between venues — panels get damaged, parts go missing and every setup starts from scratch.

EventSetupSG runs the whole programme in-house. Our warehouse, fleet and logistics team keep your assets moving between venues, and our booth setup crew assembles it to the same spec every time.

Booth Storage Between Roadshow Stops

Between venues, your booth panels, counters, LED screens and props are stored in our 600-pallet Singapore warehouse. After each teardown they're checked and inventoried, so you hear about a damaged panel before the next stop — not on setup day.

Quoting a Multi-Venue Roadshow

The fastest way to a roadshow quote is a list of venues and dates, your booth size and how long you need storage. Use the roadshow brief above or WhatsApp us your venue list — we'll return one itemised quote covering transport, setup, teardown and storage for every stop.
