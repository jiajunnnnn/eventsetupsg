LED Screen Rental in Singapore — What You Need to Know

LED screens and LED bars have become standard equipment at roadshows, brand activations and product launches across Singapore. They draw attention, display brand content dynamically, and give any activation a premium, professional look.

When renting LED screens in Singapore, the key factors to consider are the pixel pitch (which determines image clarity at different viewing distances), whether you need indoor or outdoor units, and whether your rental includes setup and teardown. A cheaper rental that doesn't include setup can end up costing more in the long run once you factor in logistics and installation.

LED Bar Rental for Roadshows and Activations

LED bars are compact, eye-catching display units that work particularly well for FMCG roadshows and product sampling. They can display looping video content, animated graphics or static product imagery — making your booth stand out in a crowded supermarket or mall environment.

We supply LED bars pre-configured with your content input options so your team can plug in and play. Setup typically takes under 30 minutes.
