What to Look for in a Booth Setup Company in Singapore

Choosing the right booth setup contractor in Singapore comes down to three things: reliability, speed, and experience with your type of activation. A roadshow booth at NTUC FairPrice has very different requirements from an exhibition build at Suntec or a brand activation at a shopping mall — and your setup team needs to know the difference.

At EventSetupSG, we have handled booth setups across every major supermarket chain, shopping mall and convention venue in Singapore. Our crew understands venue regulations, loading bay access timings, and the physical constraints of different spaces — so there are no surprises on setup day.

Roadshow Booth Setup in Singapore

Roadshow booths are the backbone of FMCG and retail brand activations in Singapore. Whether you're running a weekly promotion at Cold Storage or a product sampling at Giant, your booth needs to be set up fast, look sharp, and be dismantled cleanly every time.

We offer recurring roadshow booth setup services with dedicated crews who are familiar with your setup requirements. Consistent execution, week after week — that's what our clients rely on us for.

Exhibition Booth Setup in Singapore

Exhibition booths require more planning — structural panels, lighting rigs, display systems and branding graphics all need to come together on time at venues like Suntec City, Singapore Expo and Marina Bay Sands. We coordinate delivery, assembly and finishing so your booth is ready before the doors open.
