Event Logistics in Singapore — What It Actually Involves

Event logistics is more than just renting a truck. Moving event equipment across Singapore — especially for recurring roadshows or multi-location activations — requires coordination between warehouse staff, drivers, setup crew and venue access schedules. Getting it wrong means delayed setups, damaged assets and unhappy clients.

At EventSetupSG, we handle the full logistics chain in-house. Our warehouse team picks and packs your assets, our drivers know Singapore's venue access timings, and our setup crew are briefed on your specific requirements before they arrive on site.

Event Warehousing Singapore

Running a monthly or quarterly roadshow programme means your booth panels, LED screens and branded materials need a home between events. Our 600-pallet warehouse stores everything securely, with full inventory documentation so you always know exactly what you have and where it is.

When your next activation comes around, we pick your assets, load the truck and deliver to your venue — often within 24 hours of your request.

Multi-Location Roadshow Logistics

FMCG brands running simultaneous roadshows across multiple supermarkets or malls face a complex logistics puzzle — different venues, different timings, different crew. We manage the scheduling and routing so every location gets set up correctly and on time, every week.
